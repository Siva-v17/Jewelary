Our Story

Salem-trained, hand-finished

Stackly was founded by third-generation goldsmith Arvind Balaji, who apprenticed under his grandfather's bench before opening the atelier's doors in 2012. What started as bespoke wedding commissions for a handful of Salem families has grown into a full-line jewellery house — without changing how the work gets done.

Every design still passes through the same three hands: a designer, a caster, and a setter — before a final polish under natural light in our courtyard workshop.

Questions

Before you visit

01Do you take custom design commissions?

Yes — most bridal and heirloom-remake pieces start as a custom brief. Book an atelier visit or send a reference image through the contact page and Priya's team will follow up with a design timeline.

02What does the lifetime service include?

Every Stackly piece is covered for free resizing, re-polishing and prong tightening for as long as you own it. Bring it into the Salem atelier any time; no appointment required for routine checks.

03How do I verify a hallmark certificate?

Each piece ships with a BIS hallmark card carrying a unique HUID number, which you can verify directly on the BIS Care app. Diamonds above 0.30ct also carry an independent GIA or IGI report.

04Do you ship outside Salem?

We ship insured across India and can arrange international delivery on request. Wedding-set orders are hand-delivered within Tamil Nadu wherever possible.

05Can I trade in an old piece toward a new commission?

Yes. Bring the piece in for an in-person valuation and our team will credit its gold and stone value toward any new design or an off-the-shelf piece from the Collections page.

06Do you offer apprenticeships?

We take on a small cohort of apprentice setters and casters each year. Reach out through the contact page with a short note on your background and we'll be in touch ahead of the next intake.
