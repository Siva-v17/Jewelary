The House

Fourteen years of quiet craftsmanship

Every Stackly piece begins as a sketch on our Salem workbench. From wax carving to hand-setting, nothing leaves the atelier until it has passed under the loupe of a master craftsman three times over.

We work in small batches — rarely more than forty pieces of a single design — so that each ring, chain and pair of earrings retains the character of the hand that made it.
