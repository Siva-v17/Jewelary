How Bespoke Works

Four sittings, one piece

A bespoke commission runs across four sittings at our atelier or over video call — consultation, design approval, a wax fitting, and final delivery. Most pieces are completed within 5–7 weeks.

The Bench

From sketch to setting

Every commission passes through the same four hands before it reaches yours.

Stage 01

Design

Sketches and CAD renders, refined with you until the proportions are exact.

Stage 02

Casting

The approved design is cast in your chosen metal, then hand-filed to shape.

Stage 03

Setting

Stones are seated and secured under magnification by a senior setter.

Stage 04

Polishing

A final hand-polish and quality pass before the piece is boxed for delivery.

FAQ

Common questions

01How long does a bespoke order take?

Most commissions take 5–7 weeks from design approval to delivery, depending on stone sourcing and setting complexity.

02Do you offer certification with stones?

Yes — every diamond above 0.3ct ships with an independent grading certificate, and coloured stones include an origin report.

03Can I bring my own gold or stones?

Absolutely. We regularly rework heirloom gold and reset family stones into new designs at reduced making charges.

04What does the lifetime service cover?

Free resizing, re-polishing and prong tightening for the life of the piece — just bring it back to the atelier.
